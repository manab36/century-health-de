# dbt Project Design

```text
dbt_century_health/
  dbt_project.yml
  models/
    staging/
      stg_patients.sql
      stg_encounters.sql
      stg_conditions.sql
      stg_medications.sql
      stg_symptoms.sql
      _staging.yml
    intermediate/
      int_symptom_observations.sql
      int_encounter_conditions.sql
      int_patient_medications.sql
      _intermediate.yml
    marts/
      fct_patient_encounters.sql
      dim_patients.sql
      _marts.yml
  seeds/
  tests/
  snapshots/
```

Sources declare the five landed files/tables. Staging models rename columns, cast types, and normalize categories. Intermediate models unpack symptoms and aggregate one-to-many conditions and medications. The marts layer publishes `dim_patients` and `fct_patient_encounters`, whose grain is one row per encounter.

## Model descriptions

- `int_symptom_observations`: unpacks the packed symptom string. Its grain is one patient, pathology, and symptom name; it depends on `stg_symptoms`.
- `int_encounter_conditions`: groups condition records by encounter and collects diagnosis codes/descriptions. Its grain is one encounter; it depends on `stg_conditions`.
- `fct_patient_encounters`: joins cleaned encounters to patient attributes and pre-aggregated conditions, medications, and symptoms. Its grain is one encounter and it depends on all staging/intermediate models.

## Tests

- `stg_patients.patient_id`: `not_null` and `unique`; protects the patient dimension key.
- `stg_encounters.encounter_id`: `not_null` and `unique`; prevents duplicate visit facts.
- `fct_patient_encounters.patient_id`: `not_null`; ensures the analytical fact has a usable subject key.
- `int_symptom_observations.symptom_value`: `accepted_range` 0 to 100; catches malformed clinical scores.
- `stg_medications.end_date`: custom active-medication test allowing null or a date on/after `start_date`.
- `fct_patient_encounters.encounter_date`: `not_null`; prevents undated facts from entering time-series analysis.
